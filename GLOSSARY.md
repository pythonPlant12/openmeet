# OpenMeet

OpenMeet is a messenger with video meetings. People chat in conversations and talk face to face in rooms.

## Language

### Rooms

**Meeting**:
A room that anyone with its link and access can join.
_Avoid_: Conference, session

**Call**:
A meeting started from a conversation and tied to that conversation's members.
_Avoid_: Call session (outside the code), ring

**Call screen**:
The in-room screen that meetings and calls share.
_Avoid_: Meeting page, room page

**Active call**:
The meeting or call you are connected to, which keeps running while you use the rest of the workspace.
_Avoid_: Current room, ongoing session

**Mini call**:
The small floating view of the active call shown while the call screen is collapsed.
_Avoid_: PiP, call bubble, Voice connected panel

**Grid layout**:
The call screen layout that gives every tile equal space.
_Avoid_: Gallery

**Speaker layout**:
The call screen layout with one large tile and the other tiles in a strip. The large tile shows the pinned person, or else the person speaking.
_Avoid_: Spotlight, focus, theater mode

**Pin**:
Your own choice of who fills the large tile in the speaker layout. Only you see it.
_Avoid_: Spotlight (for everyone), feature

**Camera preview**:
The pop-up that shows your camera before you turn it on for others.
_Avoid_: Pre-join, lobby, join dialog

### People and groups

**Profile**:
A person's page: their picture, name, nickname, presence, and the actions you can take with them.
_Avoid_: Contact card, user info, profile info

**Group info**:
A group's page: its picture, title, members, and the actions you can take in it.
_Avoid_: Group details, group settings

**Presence**:
Whether a person is online, away, busy, or offline, shown as the coloured border of their picture.
_Avoid_: Online tag, status dot

### Conversations

**Chat view**:
One of the three filters of the conversations list: Chats (all), Group chats, and Friends chats.
_Avoid_: Tab, mode, filter
