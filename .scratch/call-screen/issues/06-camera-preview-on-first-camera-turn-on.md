# 06: Camera preview on first camera turn-on

**What to build:** The first time a person turns the camera on, on a given device, the camera preview opens: a video preview with rounded corners, Microphone and Camera selects over its bottom edge, and a whitish Turn on camera bubble below it. Tapping outside closes it and leaves the camera off. After the first time, the camera turns on directly, and the preview opens only from Preview camera in the camera menu.

**Blocked by:** 03 (Enter the call without a join dialog), 05 (Split mic and camera buttons).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] First camera turn-on opens the preview; later ones turn the camera on directly.
- [ ] Device selects change the preview live and are saved.
- [ ] Turn on camera turns it on for the others; tapping outside cancels.
- [ ] Preview camera opens it at any time.
- [ ] Component test for the first-time rule and both exits.
