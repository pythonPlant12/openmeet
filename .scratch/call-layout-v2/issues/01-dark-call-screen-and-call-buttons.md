# 01: Dark call screen and call buttons

**What to build:** The call screen is always dark (meetings and calls, phone and desktop) while the rest of the workspace stays light. All call buttons share one style: a squircle between a rounded square and a circle, grey glass fill; off = dark red-tinted fill with a red icon; camera on = white fill with a dark icon; leave stays red. The camera preview's buttons use the same grey fill. Spec stories 1–8.
